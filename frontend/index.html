Why use a graph database?

SkillGraph models people, skills, projects, jobs and companies as connected entities. Instead of treating every record independently, the application traverses relationships to discover meaningful career matches.

Person → HAS_SKILL → Skill → REQUIRED_FOR → Job → AT → Company
